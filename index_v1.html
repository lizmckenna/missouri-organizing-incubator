01 — Mission

Movements are how ordinary people change history — and yet most of what we know about why they succeed lives as folk wisdom, not findings.

We work alongside organizers to turn lived practice into rigorous evidence: testing tactics, training the next generation of movement scientists, and putting what we learn back into the hands of the people doing the work.

Three ways
we move the field.

A research lab, a training pipeline, and an incubator — designed to compound. What organizers learn in one feeds directly into the next.

— 01

Field experiments with organizers in the loop

We co-design studies with practitioners on questions they actually need answered: what gets people to show up, stay, and bring others.

— 02

A pipeline of movement-literate scholars
